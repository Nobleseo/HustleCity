Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Hustle City.

Is Hustle City free?
Yes, 100% free. No signup, no fees, no ads. Open the link and play. It works on any phone with a browser.
Can i advertise my business in the game?
Yes, you can send us a mail at hello@ubswift.com (state your business details, price and location--- we will reply within 5 hours with cost and modalities).
How do I play?
Pick a product — tomatoes, pepper, or wrappers. Customers walk in. Tap or drag a customer to call them to your stall, then tap the correct item on your shelf to make the sale. Serve 3 in a row to raise your combo multiplier (up to x5). The round lasts 60 seconds.
Do I need to sign up?
No. You can play 3 free rounds every day as a guest. Your wallet and scores are saved on your device. Google sign-in is coming soon — that will let you save your progress across devices.
How does the leaderboard work?
There are 3 boards: Today (resets daily at 00:00 WAT), My Division (lifetime, grouped by earning tier), and Hall of Fame (all-time net worth). A new player can win Today on day one — everyone starts at zero every morning.
What are divisions?
Divisions group players by lifetime earnings: Street Trader → Stall Owner → Market King → Mogul → Tycoon. You only move up in this version. Divisions keep competition fair — a beginner never has to beat a veteran on the same board.
What happens to my money?
Your wallet persists forever. Spend it in the Upgrade Shop on Bigger Shelf (+1 product slot) and Loudspeaker (+1.5s customer patience). Your daily score resets every morning — that's what the leaderboard uses.
Why did my score reset?
Your daily score resets at 00:00 WAT every day. That's by design — it keeps the Today leaderboard competitive. Your wallet and lifetime earnings never reset.
Why can I only play 3 rounds a day?
To keep the daily leaderboard fair for everyone. Rounds refill at midnight WAT. If you want more, you'll be able to earn Boost Tokens by inviting friends — a token starts your next round at x2 multiplier.
Can I play offline?
Yes. Once you've opened the game once, it works offline. Scores earned offline get queued and uploaded when you reconnect.
Does the game use my data?
We collect anonymous gameplay events (round started, round ended, votes) to improve the game. No personal data is collected for guest players. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Can I suggest a new hustle?
Yes! On the home screen, tap VOTE on any locked hustle (Landlord, Tailor, Buka, POS), or type your idea in the box below them. We read every single one.
How do I report a bug or give feedback?
After a round, tap "Give feedback" on the result screen. Or email hello@ubswift.com. We're listening.
Is this real money?
No. All earnings in Hustle City are virtual Naira — they have no cash value and can't be withdrawn.(we will open up that option in future updates) The game is free and there will be in game contest for prizes in future updates!
